ArrowBaby — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

ArrowBaby is an offline-first arrow puzzle. You tap, the arrows fly, the board clears, and none of your personal information ever comes to us. This policy explains what the game keeps on your phone and what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may process.

1. In short

  • No sign-in, no account and no email address are needed to play.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell any personal information.
  • We have no game servers, no leaderboards and no analytics of our own.
  • Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data (see section 4).

2. What stays on your phone

To remember your game, ArrowBaby saves the following only on your device:

  • Profile nicknames: up to 5 names you type in. Use any nickname you like; it never has to be a real name.
  • Progress for each profile: unlocked levels, stars, coins and levels cleared.
  • Settings: sound effects, music and haptics on or off.

This data is never sent to us. You can rename or delete profiles, or reset a profile, at any time in Settings. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage erases everything. If Android's backup is turned on, your phone may include this game data in your personal Google backup, which is managed by you and Google, not by us.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: used only for the little haptic taps during play. You can switch it off with the Haptics toggle.
  • Internet and network state: added by the Google ads library so ads can load. Puzzles themselves work without a connection.

ArrowBaby does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

4. Advertising by Google AdMob

The game is free and shows banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch a video for +3 hearts"). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • The advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and the general location that can be inferred from it
  • Device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad events such as impressions and taps, plus crash and performance data for the ad SDK

Google handles this data under its own policies. We never receive anything from Google that could identify you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → About & legal → Privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Coins and purchases

Coins are earned only by playing and are spent on hints. They have no real-world value, cannot be bought, sold or exchanged, and the game has no in-app purchases. No payment information is ever requested.

6. Sharing and selling

We don't collect your personal information, so we have nothing to share or sell. Google AdMob, as described above, is the only third-party service built into the game.

7. Children

ArrowBaby is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Parents: profile nicknames stay on the device, and we suggest children use a nickname instead of their real name. If a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Emails you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and never add you to any mailing list.

9. Changes

If a future update changes how data is handled, we will update this policy in the game and change the "Last updated" date.
