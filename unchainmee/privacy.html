UnchainMee — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

UnchainMee is an offline tangle puzzle. You untangle the chains, the doors open, and none of your personal information ever comes to us. This policy explains what the game keeps on your phone and what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may process.

1. In short

  • No sign-in, no account and no email address are needed to play.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell any personal information.
  • We have no game servers, no leaderboards and no analytics of our own.
  • Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data (see section 4).

2. What stays on your phone

To remember your game, UnchainMee saves the following only on your device:

  • Player nicknames: up to 5 names you type in. Use any nickname you like; it never has to be a real name.
  • Progress for each player: unlocked levels, stars and best results on each level.
  • Settings: sound effects, music and haptics on or off.

This data is never sent to us. On Android it is not included in Android backups; on iPhone it stays in the game's own storage. You can add, switch or delete players and reset progress at any time in Settings. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage erases everything.

3. Permissions and sensors

  • Vibration: used only for haptic feedback during play. You can switch it off with the Haptics toggle.
  • Internet and network state: used by the Google ads library so ads can load. The puzzles themselves work without a connection.
  • Motion sensor: while a level is on screen, the game reads the phone's tilt so the chains can sway and swing. These readings are used instantly to animate the chains and are never stored or sent anywhere.

iPhone: no special permission is needed for haptics or the internet. Before any personalised ads, the game asks once, with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether its ads may use your iPhone's advertising identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, just less personalised ones, and the game plays exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

UnchainMee does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

4. Advertising by Google AdMob

The game is free and shows banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch a short video for +20 moves"). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • The advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and the general location that can be inferred from it
  • Device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad events such as impressions and taps, plus crash and performance data for the ad SDK

Google handles this data under its own policies. We never receive anything from Google that could identify you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads).

iPhone: you can stop apps from using the advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and limit personalised ads from Apple in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

5. Purchases

UnchainMee has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment information. Stars, unlocked levels and extra moves are earned only by playing and have no real-world value.

6. Sharing and selling

We don't collect your personal information, so we have nothing to share or sell. Google AdMob, as described above, is the only third-party service built into the game.

7. Children

UnchainMee is a game for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Parents: player nicknames stay on the device, and we suggest children use a nickname instead of their real name. If a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Emails you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and never add you to any mailing list.

9. Changes

If a future update changes how data is handled, we will update this policy in the game and change the "Last updated" date.
