Accello — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Accello is a fast neon puzzle: trace the pulse from start to target before the timer runs out. Your runs, streaks and stars are yours alone. We don't collect personal information, and this policy explains what stays on your phone and what our ad partner, Google AdMob, may process.

1. QUICK SUMMARY

  • No account, login or email address is needed to play.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell your personal information.
  • We run no game servers and no analytics of our own.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data (see section 5).

2. STORED ONLY ON YOUR PHONE

To keep track of your game, Accello saves these only on your device:

  • Profile call signs: up to 5 nicknames you choose. A made-up name works perfectly.
  • Progress for each difficulty (Breezy, Extreme, Nightmare, Oblivion): levels, stars and levels cleared.
  • Stats: win streaks, perfect levels, games played, best times and unlocked achievements.
  • Daily records: the date you last played, your login streak and the daily challenges you completed.
  • Energy (⚡) balance, and the level to resume if you leave mid-run.

Dates are read from your phone's clock only to count streaks and unlock the daily challenge. Nothing here is ever sent to us. You can rename, reset or delete profiles in Stats, and uninstalling or clearing the app's storage erases everything. If Android backup is on, your phone may include this data in your personal Google backup, which you and Google control.

3. PRACTICE RANKING

The Practice Ranking in Stats is an offline, simulated leaderboard. It compares your total stars with a fixed list of sample player names built into the game. Your name and score are never uploaded or shown to anyone else, and the list does not contain other players' data.

4. PERMISSIONS

  • Vibration: for haptic feedback on taps, wins and misses.
  • Internet and network state: used only so ads can load. Puzzles play fully offline.

Accello never asks for your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

5. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

Accello is free and shows banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch a video to get energy") from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostics for the ad SDK

Google processes this under its own policies. We never receive information that identifies you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Stats → About & legal → Privacy choices.

To limit personalised ads or delete your advertising ID, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

6. ENERGY

Energy (⚡) is earned through play or by watching an ad, and is spent on retries. It has no real-world value and can't be bought, sold or exchanged. Accello has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment details.

7. SHARING AND SELLING

We don't collect personal information, so there is nothing for us to share or sell. Google AdMob is the only third-party service inside the game.

8. CHILDREN

Accello is made for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Parents: call signs stay on the device, and we recommend kids use a nickname rather than a real name. If a child has emailed us personal details, let us know and we will delete them.

9. EMAILING US

If you write to us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We never add you to a mailing list.

10. CHANGES

If an update changes how data is handled, we'll update this policy in the game and its "Last updated" date.
