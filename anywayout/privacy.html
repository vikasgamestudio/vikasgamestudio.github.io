AnywayOut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

AnywayOut is a sliding-tile escape puzzle: shift the tiles, open the path and find your way out before the moves run dry. Your route out is your business. We don't collect personal information, and this policy explains what the game keeps on your phone and what our ad partner, Google AdMob, may process.

1. AT A GLANCE

  • No sign-up, login or email address needed to play.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell your personal information.
  • We have no game servers, online leaderboards or analytics of our own.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data (see section 4).

2. KEPT ON YOUR PHONE ONLY

AnywayOut remembers your game by saving these on your device:

  • Profile names: up to 5 nicknames you choose. Any made-up name is fine.
  • Progress on each difficulty (Breezy, Extreme, Nightmare, Oblivion) and your selected difficulty.
  • Coins, stars earned, total levels cleared and daily challenges completed.
  • The date of your last daily challenge, so the next one unlocks tomorrow.

The daily puzzle is built from today's date on your phone, so everyone gets the same board without anything being sent online. None of this data reaches us. You can rename, reset or delete profiles in Stats, and uninstalling or clearing the app's storage erases everything. If Android backup is on, your phone may include this data in your personal Google backup, which you and Google control.

3. PERMISSIONS

  • Vibration: for haptic feedback as tiles slide and puzzles are solved.
  • Internet and network state: only so ads can load. Every puzzle plays offline.

AnywayOut never asks for your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

4. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

AnywayOut is free thanks to banner, interstitial and app-open ads from Google AdMob. An ad also plays when you choose "+20 moves" after running out. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostics for the ad SDK

Google processes this under its own policies. We never receive information that identifies you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Stats → About & legal → Privacy choices.

To limit personalised ads or delete your advertising ID, go to your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

5. COINS

Coins are earned by clearing levels. They have no real-world value and can't be bought, sold or exchanged. AnywayOut has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment details.

6. SHARING AND SELLING

We don't collect personal information, so there is nothing for us to share or sell. Google AdMob is the only third-party service inside the game.

7. CHILDREN

AnywayOut is made for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Parents: profile names stay on the device, and we suggest kids use a nickname. If a child has emailed us personal details, tell us and we will delete them.

8. EMAILING US

If you contact us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We never add you to a mailing list.

9. CHANGES

If an update changes how data is handled, we'll update this policy in the game and its "Last updated" date.
